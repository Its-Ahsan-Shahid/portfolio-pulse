                  How Portfolio Pulse works

                  01

                  Fetch

                  Pulls up to 25 days of company news from financial news APIs, with the most recent 5 days marked as a priority window for recency-weighted analysis.

                  02

                  Relevance & NER filter

                  Named-entity recognition plus ticker-level relevance scoring discards stories that merely mention the company in passing.

                  03

                  Materiality scoring

                  Each story is scored on financial relevance — what matters to a portfolio manager — not raw positive/negative sentiment. Only material news survives.

                  04

                  Embedding & retrieval

                  Material articles are embedded into a vector database so the agents retrieve evidence by semantic similarity, not keyword matching.

                  05

                  Agent debate

                  A bull agent builds the upside case, a bear agent builds the downside case, each citing its evidence. A moderator adjudicates into a final verdict with confidence.
